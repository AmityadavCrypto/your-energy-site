Subsidy

PM Surya Ghar subsidy guide for 2026

A clear route through current residential subsidy amounts, eligibility, documents, vendor selection, inspection, and disbursement.

By Your Energy Editorial Team8 min read

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana supports eligible households that install grid-connected rooftop solar. The subsidy can reduce the household's initial outlay, but it is not an instant discount at the quotation stage. Registration, technical approval, installation, inspection, and bank verification all matter.

Quick answer

The currently published central assistance is ₹30,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system, and ₹78,000 for a 3 kW or larger eligible residential system. Assistance is capped at the 3 kW level. Always confirm the latest amount and eligibility on the official portal before paying a vendor.

Current subsidy structure

Installed capacityPublished central assistanceWhat to remember
1 kW₹30,000Subject to scheme eligibility and successful process completion.
2 kW₹60,000The amount is cumulative for the first 2 kW.
3 kW or more₹78,000The central assistance does not keep increasing beyond the 3 kW cap.

A 5 kW household system may still be appropriate when the sanctioned load, consumption, and roof support it, but the published central subsidy remains capped at ₹78,000. System size should therefore be chosen from the property's energy needs, not only from the subsidy ceiling.

Who should check eligibility?

The programme is designed for residential consumers. A household should confirm that the electricity connection, roof rights, identity records, bank account, and portal details can be matched. Commercial and industrial connections should not assume that residential central assistance applies.

  • The applicant should have a residential electricity connection in their name or be able to complete the process accepted by the relevant DISCOM.
  • The proposed roof should be usable, structurally suitable, and reasonably free from persistent shade.
  • The system and installation should follow the programme's technical requirements.
  • The vendor and equipment route should comply with the official portal and applicable guidelines.
  • The applicant should not treat an informal promise of subsidy as approval.

The application journey

1. Register on the official portal

Start through the official PM Surya Ghar portal and select the correct state, DISCOM, consumer number, mobile number, and email. A typing error here can create avoidable delays later.

2. Apply for rooftop solar

Submit the requested consumer and system details. The DISCOM reviews technical feasibility, including connection and capacity considerations. Do not proceed on assumptions when feasibility approval is still pending.

3. Select a compliant vendor and scope

Compare quotations line by line. Confirm panel and inverter models, structure, electrical protections, earthing, warranties, monitoring, installation scope, and who will coordinate portal and metering work.

4. Complete installation and documentation

The installation should match the approved capacity and submitted details. Keep invoices, equipment serial numbers, photographs, warranties, test reports, and payment records organized.

5. Inspection, meter work, and commissioning

The DISCOM process can include inspection and replacement or installation of the required meter. The exact workflow and timing can vary, so follow the current instructions shown for your DISCOM.

6. Submit bank details for disbursement

After successful commissioning and portal completion, the applicant provides bank information as required. Names and account details should match the supporting records. The subsidy is then processed through the scheme workflow rather than deducted automatically by the website or installer.

Documents worth preparing early

  • Latest electricity bill and consumer/account number.
  • Applicant identity and mobile number used for registration.
  • Bank account details and a cancelled cheque or other requested proof.
  • Roof ownership or authorization records if requested.
  • Quotation, invoice, payment record, equipment details, and installation photographs.

Five mistakes that create problems

  1. Choosing capacity only to maximize subsidy instead of checking actual consumption and roof area.
  2. Paying for a project before technical feasibility or portal requirements are understood.
  3. Accepting a quotation that does not clearly name equipment, protections, installation scope, and exclusions.
  4. Using mismatched names or bank details across the bill, application, and account.
  5. Assuming a vendor can guarantee the government's approval or disbursement date.
Policy note

Scheme rules, portal screens, technical requirements, and DISCOM processes can change. Verify the current position on the official PM Surya Ghar portal and with your DISCOM before making a financial commitment.
