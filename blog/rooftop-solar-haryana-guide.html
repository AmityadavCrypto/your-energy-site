Rooftop solar in Haryana: what to plan first

A homeowner-friendly route through consumption, feasibility, roof design, permissions, metering, and a safe handover.

By Your Energy Editorial Team2 August 20267 min read

From urban homes in Gurgaon and Faridabad to properties across Rewari, Narnaul, Mahendragarh, Bhiwani, Charkhi Dadri, and Kund, the right rooftop solar design begins with the same principle: match the system to the property instead of buying a generic capacity package.

Quick answer

Check a year of bills, sanctioned load, roof ownership, shade, structural condition, inverter location, cable route, and the current DISCOM application process. Finalize capacity only after these checks agree.

Build a reliable consumption picture

A single high summer bill can overstate normal demand, while one low winter bill can understate it. Use twelve months where possible. Separate unit consumption from the rupee amount because tariff slabs, fixed charges, arrears, and adjustments can make two similar bills look different.

Also list planned loads. An additional air conditioner, rental floor, water pump, home office, or electric vehicle can change the useful capacity. For a business, record operating hours and weekday versus weekend demand.

Survey the roof properly

  • Measure usable area after excluding tanks, mumties, parapets, vents, antennas, and access paths.
  • Observe shade from neighbouring buildings and trees at different times.
  • Identify whether the roof is reinforced concrete, sheet roofing, or another construction.
  • Plan structure anchoring, waterproofing, wind resistance, and safe maintenance access.
  • Choose an inverter location protected from direct sun, rain, dust concentration, and poor ventilation.
  • Trace the AC and DC cable route and the location of earthing and protection equipment.

Choose system type from the actual need

An on-grid system is normally designed to work with the utility grid and does not provide ordinary backup during a power cut. An off-grid or hybrid design adds backup capability but also adds batteries, controls, cost, space, and replacement considerations. If backup is important, list critical loads and required backup hours rather than simply requesting “hybrid solar.”

Follow the current approval process

For eligible residential subsidy cases, use the official national portal and follow the relevant Haryana DISCOM workflow. For other projects, confirm the current HERC framework and DISCOM requirements. Older rooftop regulations or online articles may no longer reflect the operative position, so a dated screenshot or old checklist should not be treated as current approval guidance.

  1. Confirm the bill details, connection category, sanctioned load, and serving DISCOM.
  2. Submit the application through the official channel and wait for technical feasibility where required.
  3. Install only after the proposed scope and approval route are clear.
  4. Keep equipment records, invoices, photographs, test documents, and warranties.
  5. Complete inspection, meter work, commissioning, and portal updates.

Demand a complete handover

A finished installation should come with more than a switched-on inverter. Ask for the single-line diagram where applicable, module and inverter data, serial numbers, warranty documents, monitoring access, labels, test or commissioning records, shutdown instructions, cleaning guidance, and service contacts.

Red flags in a proposal

  • Guaranteed generation without a shade study or stated assumptions.
  • No exact module or inverter model.
  • No description of structure, earthing, protections, cable, or civil work.
  • Subsidy represented as guaranteed cash or an immediate installer discount.
  • No local service ownership after installation.
  • Pressure to pay before approval responsibilities and exclusions are explained.
Regulatory note

HERC regulations and DISCOM processes are updated over time. Verify the current official position before application, installation, or payment.
