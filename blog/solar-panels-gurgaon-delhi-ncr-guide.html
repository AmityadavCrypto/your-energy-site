Solar panels in Gurgaon and Delhi NCR

A roof-first guide to choosing capacity and equipment for dense urban properties, seasonal demand, dust, and local approval requirements.

By Your Energy Editorial Team6 min read

Rooftop solar can work well across Gurgaon, Faridabad, and the wider Delhi NCR region, but urban roofs often have constraints that a quick satellite image cannot reveal. Water tanks, mumties, neighbouring towers, trees, shared access, and future construction can all change the usable area.

Quick answer

Do not select panels from wattage or brand alone. First establish usable shade-free area, annual consumption, daytime demand, sanctioned load, system type, structure, electrical safety, approvals, and service responsibility.

Why a physical survey matters in NCR

Closely spaced buildings create moving shade. A roof may appear open at noon but lose morning or afternoon production. Measure around permanent obstacles, allow access for tank maintenance and cleaning, and review whether nearby construction could create future shade.

For apartment societies and commercial buildings, also clarify roof rights, common-area loads, fire and access considerations, decision-making authority, and how the project will connect to the correct meter.

Use annual units, not only the bill amount

Electricity bills reflect more than energy usage. Fixed charges, tariff slabs, taxes, and adjustments affect the rupee total. Collect monthly units for a full year and note when the property is occupied. A family that is away during the day has a different self-consumption pattern from a clinic, school, shop, or office operating in daylight hours.

Design for dust and extreme seasons

NCR experiences dusty periods, high summer temperatures, winter fog, and monsoon weather. The installation should provide ventilation under modules, weather-suitable components, proper cable management, safe roof drainage, and a cleaning path. Monitoring should be used to detect a sustained performance change rather than assuming every low day is a fault.

Panel comparison: what deserves attention

  • Exact model: the quotation should identify a model, not just a brand and wattage.
  • Technology and efficiency: helpful when roof area is limited, but not a substitute for system design.
  • Temperature behaviour: relevant to real operating conditions.
  • Product and performance warranty: understand what each covers and who handles a claim.
  • Mechanical ratings: should fit the project's structural and wind design.
  • Traceability and service: serial numbers and authorized supply records help after installation.

Do not overlook the balance of system

Modules are only one part of a safe project. The inverter, mounting structure, DC and AC cables, connectors, isolators, protective devices, earthing, lightning protection where required, conduit, labels, monitoring, meter coordination, and workmanship influence reliability.

Approval and subsidy checks

The route depends on the location, serving DISCOM, connection category, proposed capacity, and whether a residential applicant is seeking central assistance. Use current official portals rather than relying on an old online checklist. Confirm who will submit, track, and correct the application.

A useful quotation should state

  • Proposed capacity and the consumption assumptions behind it.
  • Exact panel and inverter makes and models.
  • Structure specification and roof fixing method.
  • Electrical safety scope and earthing arrangement.
  • Expected generation assumptions, without a misleading guarantee.
  • Approval, metering, civil work, and waterproofing responsibilities.
  • Payment milestones, exclusions, warranties, and service contacts.

Sources and verification

  • MNRE: Grid Connected Rooftop Solar Programme
  • MNRE: residential central financial assistance guidelines
