Gurugram FAQs

Do you install solar for both homes and businesses in Gurugram?

Yes. We support residential, commercial, institutional, and housing-society projects, with system planning based on each property's actual load and roof conditions.

Can Your Energy help with residential subsidy guidance?

Yes. We can explain the current process and documentation for eligible residential projects. Eligibility, rates, and final approval remain subject to current government and DISCOM rules.

What should I share for an initial estimate?

Start with a recent electricity bill, property type, city, approximate roof information, and your contact details. A site survey is needed before a final design and quotation.

Is the Gurugram address a real office?

Yes. It is an official Your Energy office. Please call before visiting so we can arrange the appropriate team member for your enquiry.
