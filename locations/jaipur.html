Solar for Jaipur

Different roofs need different project logic.

Jaipur combines compact residential rooftops, shared society roofs, hospitality properties, institutions, and large commercial sites. Each needs a different balance of load matching, roof planning, approvals, safety, and ongoing maintenance.

01

Homes and societies

Assess bills, terraces, common loads, committee requirements, shading, meter arrangements, and current residential subsidy conditions.

02

Hospitality and institutions

Review daytime and seasonal demand, occupied-site safety, equipment placement, service continuity, monitoring, and maintenance access.

03

Commercial and industrial

Connect tariff, operating profile, sanctioned load, roof structure, generation assumptions, project economics, and compliance responsibilities.

The Jaipur process

A solar project people can review and operate.

Our process keeps the engineering, financial assumptions, execution scope, and long-term ownership plan visible at each stage.

01

Energy and objective review

Understand bills, tariff, consumption pattern, future demand, property use, and commercial priorities.

02

Roof and electrical survey

Inspect usable area, structure, shadows, access, drainage, panels, metering, and cable routes.

03

Engineering and proposal

Present sizing, layout, equipment, generation basis, commercials, scope, approvals, and milestones.

04

Execution and lifecycle care

Coordinate safety, installation, testing, handover, monitoring, maintenance, and service support.
