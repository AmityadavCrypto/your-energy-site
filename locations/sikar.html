Sikar FAQs

Clarity before equipment selection.

The right inverter or panel cannot be chosen responsibly without understanding the load, roof, and project objectives.

Do you support institutional solar projects in Sikar?

Yes. We can assess suitable schools, colleges, clinics, offices, and other institutions based on daytime consumption, roof conditions, safety, and project objectives.

How do you plan for dusty rooftop conditions?

The survey and design discussion should consider safe cleaning access, water availability, roof traffic, module layout, and a realistic maintenance schedule.

Can you help explain residential subsidy steps?
