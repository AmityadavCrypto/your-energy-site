Solar for Rewari

Built for independent roofs and working properties.

Rewari-area projects can range from family homes and roadside businesses to schools, workshops, and larger operating sites. The useful question is not only how much roof exists, but when and how electricity is consumed.

01

Homes and farmhouses

Plan around monthly bills, daytime consumption, terrace use, shading, water tanks, future loads, and applicable residential schemes.

02

Shops and enterprises

Review operating hours, sanctioned load, roof usability, equipment demand, and savings assumptions before final sizing.

03

Schools and institutions

Match daytime generation with recurring institutional loads while planning safe access, monitoring, and maintenance responsibilities.

What kinds of projects do you handle around Rewari?

We support independent homes, shops, offices, institutions, warehouses, workshops, and other suitable residential or commercial properties.

Is Kund a separate Your Energy office?

No. Our physical office is at the Rewari address shown on this page. Kund is one of the areas served from this regional network.

Can I get a quotation without a site survey?

We can provide an initial estimate using your bill and basic property details. A final system design and quotation should follow a technical site review.

Do you provide maintenance support after installation?

Support can include monitoring guidance, cleaning and preventive-check planning, documentation, and service coordination based on the agreed project scope.
